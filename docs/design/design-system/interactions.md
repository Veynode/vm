# Interactions: conversation and status

Part of the [Veynode design system](../DESIGN.md). Conversation, composer, AI status, approvals, computers and the Bot and Group editors (section 9, first half). Where these stand on screen is the [shell](shell.md).

## 9. Veynode interactions

Interaction rules for the conversation, AI status and settings. How transcript rows are drawn, measured and scrolled is the [message layout contract](message-layout.md#message-layout-contract); every message height, loading or layout change follows it, never another observer or scroll correction. Reading position, disclosure state and drafts are part of the experience.

### Conversation and composer

- **One input surface.** The composer floats over the transcript, so it alone in the pane lifts: a soft two-layer shadow (`shadow-composer`; with focus the field ring joins it, `shadow-composer-focus`) over its hairline. The composer is one 48px pill (36px buttons, 6px inset); Send, when there is something to send, carries a faint top highlight and a small shadow (`shadow-send`) while the words fit on a line beside its buttons; it stacks once they do not (words full width on top, inset to line up with the buttons, + and Send on the row below) and unstacks only when the words are shorter than when they stacked, so the same words never flip; a placeholder never stacks it. It is flat: `surface`, a 1px `line` hairline, no shadow, the field ring while focused. Do not wrap it in another input border. It offers no model: a Bot's model account is chosen only in Edit Bot. With no model connected at all (read from `connectionQueries.all()` once it has answered) one status line above the words says so with Connect, which opens the same `SetupModelConnection` dialog as the Bot editor. A send the Controller still refuses for it (`MODEL_NOT_CONNECTED`) is said in the person's language as a toast whose one action is Connect a model, which opens Settings → Models.
- **One motion for every change of shape** (a line added or removed, stacking, the quoted line arriving, Send emptying it), owned by `useComposerMorph`: the box eases to its new height from the bottom up over 200ms, each `[data-morph]` part slides from where it was to where it goes on the same curve, a part that grew is uncovered from its top, so the words never scroll to chase the caret. A change mid-motion starts from what is on screen; reduced motion snaps. While any composer height animation runs, `VirtualMessages` writes each frame's inset straight to the list (end padding, spacer height, `--composer-inset` for the rail and Jump to latest), holds a follower at the end in the same frame, and commits `composerInset` once when the motion ends; never re-render the list per frame.
- **Reply** puts the message answered inside the composer above the editor as a 44px `surface-2` plate (`lg` corners, concentric with the composer's): a 32px round `surface` plate with the still reply icon, one line (the sender's name in `ink-2`, then the quoted message with an ellipsis) and ×. It fades in as the composer grows, and pressing it finds the original. The placeholder reads `composer.replyPlaceholder`; answering a question keeps its own placeholder and the line is the question's title.
- **Files** staged for the next message sit in one row above the editor (`ComposerFiles`), in the order added, at most five. The row scrolls sideways without a scrollbar: a wheel scrolls it too, the edge that hides more fades, an arrow over that edge pages it, and a file added scrolls to the end. Every file is 56px tall: a picture is a square thumbnail, any other file a row of its type sticker, its name truncated in the middle so the extension stays, and its size, or the upload's percent while it uploads. Drafts have a corner dismiss control; while uploading, a stroke traces progress around the tile, and dismiss (hover or focus) cancels. The person's own sent files are one-line rows above their bubble in their fill, sticker and name, a press previews; their pictures stand above the bubble too. A picture that does not load shows its icon only, no words. Skills chosen with / remain editor tokens. Incoming images, file chips and delivered-file lists keep their presentation. Keep three inline file previews and the View all dialog; never expand file lists into long rows.
- **One control, Send, that never changes shape**: a flat ink circle when there is something to send, `field` grey with an `ink-3` arrow when not. No stop button, spinner or animated rim while a Bot works; a message written during a reply joins the Run under way. A send in flight is the message itself at 60% opacity until it lands. Stop belongs to the progress line: a quiet underlined Stop right after its label, while the person's reply runs and never while it is only queued (Escape also stops it); when the label rolls to another length its width eases on the roll's curve so Stop slides with it. The composer empties the moment Send is pressed (words, files, skills and reply target leave with the message); a refusal gives them back unless the person already started the next message. The transcript fades out under the composer over the 28px above it; the fade hangs off `.composer-frame`, outside the form.
- **Overlays above the Group avatar rail**: the history notice and Jump to latest report on the whole transcript, so nothing inside it may cover them. The notice is the toast's ink pill (Reconnecting… with the waiting mark, or a failed read with the failed mark, with one text action: Reconnect or Load older messages).
- **Jump to latest** is an icon-only round `IconButton` (`aria-label` from `virtualMessages.jumpToLatest`), 40px, a floating layer: translucent `surface` over a backdrop blur, a 1px `line` hairline, `shadow-float`. It rises 12px on the pop curve over 200ms and leaves on the exit curve in 120ms. It appears only when the reader is outside the follow band or newer messages exist, is positioned above the measured composer height and must stay hit-testable there. When a reply, card or delivery lands while the reader is above the band (including past the window pinned for a reader who left the end) it carries a small `badge-new` dot (`data-fresh-reply`, `aria-hidden`) until they reach the end or press it; a row that draws nothing never lights it. The label stays Jump to latest.
- **Keyboard and input**: preserve send and newline shortcuts, paste and attachments. Enter during IME composition must not send; never cut composition events or override OS shortcuts.
- **A diagram can be enlarged.** A Mermaid diagram sits in a fixed 240px box in its reply ([Markdown blocks](boardui.md#boardui-contract)); clicking the box, or the enlarge button in its header (reachable by keyboard, named `markdownMessage.enlargeDiagram`), opens the full-size drawing in a dialog over the transcript. Closing it returns focus to where it was and moves nothing in the transcript. Tables and code are not enlarged: they scroll inside their boxes.
- **Replies are whole.** There is no details view behind a reply: what the Bot is doing is the progress slot, what it asked is a card, what it did is its reply. The reply context menu offers only what the reader can act on (Copy, Reply, and Check and continue on a failed Run); no Regenerate, no details or overflow button. Reply and Copy sit beside the bubble's top; on a message taller than the screen they are `position: sticky` inside an absolute column as tall as the bubble, following the reader just under the header band (`--header-inset` + 8px) and stopping at the bubble's foot; nothing is measured on scroll, so no row changes height.
- Approval, clarification and takeover content stays actionable. Do not duplicate one failure as both an error bubble and an action card.
- **A failure is said once, in the Bot's place** (`attachRunFailures`). `RunFailure` on the Run's own row, where it ended, draws the sentence as one of the Bot's bubbles (`MessageNotice` `align="bubble"`): an outlined bubble under the Bot's face and name, the sentence in `ink-2`, its recovery actions (Retry, Settings, Open computer) on a line under it, and a close button on its first line. A Group member's failure is that member's bubble, its Bot's name and face in the name row above it like any message, never inside the bubble. A retry says nothing of its own: the first Run's bubble follows it while it works and when it fails too, and gives way to its reply once it is done, in the failure's place. Interrupted work shows only its Continue bubble, and the Computer's closing line for a Run that stopped short (`<runId>.stopped`, written for the model) is never rendered.
- **System lines.** What happened to a Run itself (set aside, resumed) is `MessageNotice` with `align="center"`: one caption centred across the whole row with its 14px symbol, no chip, `ink-2`, a failure in the failed ink, waiting in the waiting ink, its action in bold ink (Continue, Retry). The row (`chat-exchange`) steps back over its column through `--column-offset`. Lines belonging to one message stay with it (a failed send under the person's message on the right, a stopped reply under the Bot's words on the left). A line says what happened and stops; the action beside it needs no explaining sentence.
- **Finder and search.** The sidebar has no filter field. Its search button (and ⌘K / Ctrl+K wherever no modal is open) opens `ConversationFinder`: a dialog over the conversations in the sidebar's order, narrowed by name as the person types; arrows move, Enter opens through the same path as pressing a row, ⌘1–⌘9 (Ctrl elsewhere) open the first nine without leaving the field, Escape closes and returns focus. ⌘F stays the in-conversation message search (`ConversationSearch`), which the chat header's search button also opens.
- The details pane's editor footer centres on the composer's line: the composer keeps its place and the footer's padding follows it.
- **List previews** never prefix a state the avatar already shows: only running, waiting, queued, failed and paused earn a word. Row data is indexed once per state change, never rescanned per row. The preview shows what the newest entry means, never diagnostics (`conversationListPreview`): a waiting approval, takeover or macOS permission row shows the card's own question in the reader's language from the same `packages/console/src/lib/card-question.ts` helpers; a sealed question never shows its words; fallbacks are catalog labels (Waiting for your approval, Waiting for your answer, Your control is needed, Reply stopped, Reply failed). Protocol prompts such as `Allow shell?`, the Computer's English prompt and failure detail never reach the list; a person's answer shows the words its button said.
- Preserve original user text, code, filenames and authorized diagnostics; server redaction and visibility limits still apply. Highlighting must not change source text or font metrics.

### AI status, approval, and computers

- Derive status from real events. Never invent percentages, steps or remaining time while waiting for a model; unconfirmed results must not appear completed.
- **A Bot never installs.** When a task needs a catalog plugin it lacks, the Bot asks in its owner's direct chat with a card (Install, Not now); the owner installs, configures and signs in through Settings' own install box, and what came of it is given to that Bot from their next message, not the task under way (see the [message layout contract](message-layout.md#message-layout-contract)).
- Approval cards explain the action and its exact target whole, in the reader's language (see the [message layout contract](message-layout.md#message-layout-contract)). Each answer covers only what its label says: this call, this kind of action for the rest of the task, or the scope named beside Always allow.
- **Opening a computer is using it**: there is no Take over, Return control or view-only mode for the person it belongs to. While the person gives input on a desktop (the viewer reports it at most once a second, `packages/console/src/lib/desktop-activity.ts`) a Bot's next click or keystroke there waits for them to stop; terminal and file work goes on. Done, carry on is the only way to say a takeover is finished; closing the window never says it, and a Run waiting on the person keeps waiting. A person the computer is only shown to (view access) gets a viewer that cannot act, with disabled controls saying so (`computerDialog.viewOnlyAccess`); button appearance does not establish permission. A computer that still takes control in turns is handed to the window as it opens and let go as it closes, with nothing to press; there Done, carry on also lets go and closes the window.
- **A screen the person sits at is never streamed back to them.** The console draws the machine card (`CurrentComputer`) in place of the picture, in the window and in the sidebar preview, whenever the computer is the machine the page is on (`isCurrentComputer`, `packages/console/src/lib/current-computer.ts`). A page served from the node itself knows it by its address; a page served through a relay (the account's web app) asks its own browser's loopback, `GET /api/node/same-machine/:id` on the web port its machine record names (`webPort`, 18421 when it names none), which the node answers (204, any origin) only to a request from this machine that names this machine's own id, so no other page learns that a node runs here. A node elsewhere stays unanswered and its screen is drawn.
- **The computer window** follows the app's theme; only the screen in it is dark whatever the theme (its frame carries `dark`, `ComputerDialog.tsx`). The header is one row ([Heads](shell.md#heads)): the window's controls at the left, `<computer> · <Bot>` (on a computer with a desktop per Bot, the computer's name and a small select of My desktop and each Bot's desktop) ended by the computer's status icon, fullscreen and close at the right. In the desktop app a computer opens in a window of its own (`openComputer`, the `/screen/<computer id>` page): the dialog fills it, the header is the title bar with the traffic lights at the left, the window's own controls replace fullscreen and close, and closing lets go of the computer first. Every action is one toolbar of icon buttons in the title row (`computerDialog.controls`), never over the picture: clipboard, Files (one popover with Upload at the top, its where-to in an ⓘ, the computer's files below newest first, each row a download, Refresh in the list header, no list when there are none), sound, microphone and the touch Keyboard. The screen's own group (the displays choice and the clipboard state, `DesktopViewerControls`) is in that toolbar too. Sound and microphone are one click on and off; hovering or focusing raises a shared panel (`DesktopDockPanels`) with volume and, where the browser can choose an output (`AudioContext.setSinkId`), the system default and each device; moving between the two buttons slides one card and cross-fades its contents (200ms, reduced motion snaps); arrow keys on the speaker change the volume. Volume and devices are remembered per device (`desktopVolumeAtom`, `desktopSpeakerAtom`, `desktopMicrophoneAtom`). Switching desktops or a new lease reconnects the screen and sound and microphone follow; the last picture of the same desktop stays up (`packages/console/src/lib/desktop-still.ts`) until the new stream has drawn, then fades into it, with no spinner. Opened for a Bot the window shows that Bot's desktop when it has one, else the person's own. Opened from the mini screen, the window starts on the mini screen's last picture (the mini stops streaming as the window opens, so it sets its frame down for the window: `shelvePicture`, `paintShelved`), with no line over it; the window's own stream replaces it through the same fade, and with no mini frame the window shows the connecting placeholder as before. A stream that drops (a network change, a lock on the Mac) keeps the last picture and tries again by itself for 30 seconds, from half a second and backing off (`packages/console/src/lib/desktop-reconnect.ts`); a working badge, Reconnecting, floats at the top of the picture and takes no room. Only when the 30 seconds pass does the placeholder show the failure with Reconnect, and a refused ticket (signed out, no access) shows it at once. A retry asks for no control, so a lease that moved on stays where it went. The mini screen retries the same way and keeps its picture without a status. A failure still shows the placeholder.
- **The computer window says when it is the person's turn.** While a Run waits on a takeover of the computer on screen the badge by the title reads Your turn · <ask> with the hint behind it (`DetailTip`), in the card's words (`takeoverQuestion` / `takeoverHint` in `card-question.ts`); Done, carry on (plus I won't pay on a payment) join the title-row controls last, so nothing else moves. Nothing under the picture says who is using the computer; a Bot at work shows as the glow around it. A start that failed shows the computer not running with Start and the reason behind the failed badge, with no toast, whenever the computer's record carries the failure; a start that failed without changing the record (a refusal, a dropped connection) is a toast naming the reason in a few words with Details and Retry. Out of date and access off are that badge too, never a line over the picture. On a touch screen, on a desktop the person may act on, a Keyboard button raises the phone's keyboard for one desktop and lease at a time.
- **A phone shows the desktop as screen and touchpad** (`PhoneDesktop`, a touch screen narrower than the `narrow` breakpoint): the screen on top, shaped like the picture, and a touchpad filling the rest: a rounded layer in the app's own theme (the screen alone stays dark) labelled "Touchpad". The viewer under the screen takes no touch, so a tap on the picture clicks nothing. One finger on the pad moves the pointer relatively (faster swipes go further, a magnified screen moves it less), a short tap clicks, a tap then a touch landing within a third of a second and 40px of it that moves drags, as on a laptop's trackpad (one that lands elsewhere or later only moves), and three fingers landing together drag too, two fingers landing together and moving scroll with the content following them (a finger landing after the first has begun to move is ignored), and two fingers tapped together right-click (`packages/console/src/lib/desktop-touchpad.ts`). Only two fingers on the screen magnify it, up to 4× around the point they pinched, which becomes the pointer, and the screen then follows the pointer (`packages/console/src/lib/desktop-touch-view.ts`); the guest's own cursor (its shapes included) is drawn at the pointer as a vector, sharp at any size and a little larger when magnified (arrow, text beam, hand, crosshair, horizontal and vertical resize, four-way move; a shape it cannot tell is drawn as the arrow; `packages/console/src/components/computer/desktop/cursor.ts`); a 1× button joins the key row while it is magnified, and "Pinch to zoom" shows once per device. One row at the foot holds the Keyboard button, which stays put, and the keys, which scroll beside it: Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt and ⌘ (they stay down for the next key typed, then let go) and the arrows. The button raises the phone's own keyboard, and the row rides above it. The window runs to the screen's edges with no frame and no fullscreen button. The viewers register what the pad can do and report their picture's size through `packages/console/src/lib/desktop-touch-bridge.ts`; every input still goes through the viewer's own gate. Landscape is not designed; the clipboard, Files, sound and microphone stay in the title row.
- Connection failure, offline state, exhausted quota and expired authorization need appropriate next steps. Details contain only permitted, redacted diagnostics. Working indicators use bounded event summaries, never full commands, inputs, paths or outputs.
- Switching Bots, conversations or details never implicitly grants control. Visual work must preserve execution and authorization semantics. Use the feature's existing cancellation protocol when stopping is needed; closing a view does not stop remote work.

### Bot and Group editors

Both open in the side pane beside the conversation (a `Modal` on a narrow screen), under the side editor's head ([Heads](shell.md#heads)), with Save in the 64px footer on the composer's line. Opened as a dialog the Bot editor takes the `Modal` size `xl` (880px), so the avatar and the fields stand side by side ([Dialog anatomy](components.md#dialog-anatomy)).

**Bot editor.** An existing Bot's editor opens with a leading Back action to the conversation details without collapsing the sidebar, and has two pill tabs (a group's has Basics, Strategy and Routines, in the same place):

- Settings: name, picture, instructions, the model (one Model service choice listing every ChatGPT account and model service alike, then the account's model), for the Bot's owner only the computer, and last Use my memories (a switch with one line of help, off for a Bot that serves other people or is a test). It is a draft until Save changes, which sends only the fields that changed and is disabled until something did. A failure keeps the draft and is said under its field (once above Save when a multi-field Save failed as a whole) and goes when the person changes what it was about. A person who does not own the Bot sees why its fields are locked. While the Bot has a task running its model and computer are locked, with the reason behind a lock mark and an ⓘ beside the locked fields (`LockTip`), so the footer keeps its line. The Routine editor's Save is disabled until something changed, like the Bot's (a new Routine saves at once). Choosing another chat over unsaved changes in a Bot, Group or Routine editor asks "Discard changes?" first (Discard, Keep editing).
- Plugins: one All switch (explained behind an info tooltip) above one list: the Skills built into Veynode folded first under Built into Veynode with how many are on (a Bot with its own list can leave them out one by one), then skills and MCP accounts grouped under the plugin they came from. Search appears only when the Bot's own items make it long; a search opens the fold and has one switch per MCP account. These switches apply as flipped. An account not yet connected cannot be switched on and leads to Settings, which only installs and connects accounts and never chooses Bots.
- A Permissions row beside Plugins opens owner-controlled default confirmations, category rules and existing tool-specific rules within the editor, a draft until Save changes. A YOLO mode switch at the bottom preserves the other permission rules when disabled; explicit denials, machine restrictions and human-control boundaries still apply.
- Creating a Bot is one form with Create, no example presets and the same fields in the same order as Settings, which then opens the new Bot's editor. The form opens the step it points to over itself, never in Settings behind it: adding a computer (New and Connect as pill tabs) and connecting a model (`SetupModelConnection`). Each is a nested Modal portaled to the page body outside the form's `<form>`, so the draft keeps what was typed; a computer created there is chosen for the draft (`assignBot={false}`) instead of being offered to a Bot on its success screen, so only the Bot's own choice binds it. The Bot form never opens a computer's own editor.
- A Bot whose model or computer was deleted is left with no model (`botHasModel`, owner decision 2026-10-24). Its face is failed wherever the face shows a state (`Avatar`); for its owner, its conversation row and the chat's name capsule carry a `waiting` badge after its name, Choose a model (`NoModelBadge`), which opens this editor at the model, as the capsule does then. Its other settings save while it has none. A send to it is refused (`MODEL_NOT_CHOSEN`) with a toast whose action is Choose a model.
- With a model connected but no Bot of the person's own, the empty workspace says so in one line and offers one primary action, Create Bot. An owned Bot without a computer gets one from its editor's Settings, and its details say nothing about the missing computer. Routines belong to Bots, not Computers, and stay available without a bound Computer; only computer-dependent work needs one.

**Group editor.** Titled with the group's name. Basics shows the members' collage, then the name, a draft that Save changes sends. Members (`GroupMembers`) apply as changed, like the plugin switches: each row shows the member and role, edited where it is read; a row's ··· menu removes it (never the last); Add Bots opens `AddBotsDialog`, a list of the person's own Bots not in the group, ticked and added together with Add (N), never on one click. The conversation details' Members heading opens the same dialog. With nobody left to add, the entry is disabled with that reason behind its ⓘ. While the team works, adding and removing are disabled with the reason said (the server refuses member changes then), while the name and roles still save and reach the room's next turn. Creating a group is one form with its Bots and roles.
