The optional signed-session mode validates paired keys on this local path. It does not validate Internet connectivity, external TURN, trusted remote browser-code delivery or complete end-to-end security.

Desktop control

Control uses the control picture below; the video above is for viewing and cannot anchor input. Every input is signed against one picture issued by the host, and a picture is usable for 4 seconds. Keys are physical keys, not text or Unicode input.



Inputs are sent one at a time, in order; up to 8 may wait and further ones are refused, never sent late. After each input the picture is fetched again. Not available on this connection: pointer hover, held keys or buttons, modifier + click, text paste and IME. Ctrl+Alt+Escape releases the keyboard from the picture. Browser-reserved shortcuts (for example Ctrl+W) never reach the page.
